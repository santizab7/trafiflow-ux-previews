The Platform

One intelligence layer behind every brand we build.

TrafiFlow is Trafilea's in-house AI platform. It unifies our data, predicts what's next, and acts on it — so our teams launch faster and scale further than a traditional operator could.

Unified data

Every channel, SKU, and customer touchpoint across our portfolio in one source of truth — normalised in real time, so decisions are made on what's actually happening.

Predictive intelligence

Our models surface demand shifts, forecast revenue, and flag creative fatigue before they show up in the numbers — turning hindsight into foresight.

Agentic execution

The platform doesn't just recommend — AI agents act. Budget shifts, audience tuning, and creative rotations run continuously, with our teams steering strategy.

The Shift

A new operating model for consumer brands.

The difference isn't another tool. It's moving from a stack of disconnected dashboards to a single, AI-native operating layer.

Capability The traditional operating model The Trafilea platform
Data Fragmented across many tools, reconciled by hand Unified in real time, one source of truth
Decisioning Backward-looking reports, debated in meetings Predictive signals, surfaced before they hit results
Execution Manual, limited to working hours Agentic and continuous, teams steer strategy
Speed to launch Constrained by headcount and tooling Compounding — each brand benefits from the last
Cost structure Stacked subscriptions and agency overhead One platform built and owned in-house

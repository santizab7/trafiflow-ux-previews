Execution is the constraint

In direct-to-consumer, results come from how well consumer insight, product, creative, media, conversion, and CRM are connected — not from any single channel. Trafilea operates that full loop on one system.

How Trafilea operates

Trafiflow — the operating system Trafilea runs on.

Trafiflow is Trafilea's internal operating system. It connects consumer insight, product, creative, media, conversion, and CRM on a single stack, so decisions are made with the full picture and executed consistently across brands. It is how a lean team operates a $405M business profitably.

Trained on first-party data

Models trained on a decade of Trafilea's own performance data across media, pricing, creative, and retention — not generic tooling.

The full funnel on one system

Insight, product, creative, media, conversion, and CRM connected end-to-end, so the whole loop is optimized together.

Execution, not just reporting

Beyond dashboards, it helps execute changes to budget, pricing, creative, and retention — under operator control.

Governed by operators

Human-in-the-loop approvals, spend and brand limits, and full auditability keep decisions accountable.

Built from years of first-party data and daily operations, it is proprietary and extremely hard to replicate.

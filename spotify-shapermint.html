01 — Why this, why now

The reach ceiling is
a concentration problem.

The board's standing concern — the Meta performance-decline hypothesis — isn't really about Meta getting worse. It's about Shapermint buying the same finite pool of in-market women, harder, at a rising price. Audio is the largest premium reach pool we have never systematically tested. Three things make June 2026 the moment to move.

The dependency
~1 channel

Meta still anchors Shapermint's prospecting. Every incremental dollar there competes against itself for the same impressions — frequency climbs, marginal CAC climbs with it. Diversification isn't a hedge; it's how we reopen efficient reach.

The unlock
SAX

The Spotify Ad Exchange went live globally in 2025 (The Trade Desk, DV360, Magnite). For the first time we can bid on logged-in Spotify listeners in a live auction with our own identity and audience data — not a fixed insertion order.

The measurement
Haus

As of Nov 2025, Haus connects Spotify Ads natively for GeoLift and cMMM. We can attribute Spotify spend to incrementality automatically — meaning a Spotify demo can be held to the same causal bar as every other channel from day one.

02 — The audience fit

Shapermint's buyer
is already listening.

Shapermint's core is women 45+ in the US — the body-confidence movement, not a trend chaser. The lazy read is "Spotify skews young." The accurate read: the ad-supported tier over-indexes female, and the 45–54 cohort alone is a double-digit share of a 380M-strong addressable base. That's tens of millions of in-demo listeners we can reach with frequency control Meta can't match.

03 — Why Spotify beats "more social"

Different attention,
not cheaper attention.

01

Captive, no-skip reach

Standard audio ads have no skip button and run when the user is in-app and listening — completion rates of 85–95%. The brand story is delivered in full, not thumb-scrolled past in 0.8 seconds.

02

Incremental, not duplicated

Spotify reaches our buyer in a context Meta and TV don't own — commuting, chores, workouts. The goal isn't to re-touch the same impressions cheaper; it's net-new reach against frequency-saturated audiences.

03

Programmatic + our data

Via SAX through The Trade Desk / DV360 we can apply UID2, PAIR and RampID — activating Shapermint first-party audiences and lookalikes against logged-in Spotify users in a live auction.

04

Causal measurement built in

Haus GeoLift turns the demo into a designed experiment: hold out geos, read true incremental lift, and feed validated results into cMMM — the same standard we hold every channel to.

04 — What we'd actually run

Three formats,
one funnel.

A layered test: audio carries reach and the emotional brand story, video captures the in-app visible session, and podcast lends host-credibility to the body-confidence message. All measurable, all bought through Ads Manager / SAX.

Primary · Reach

Audio ads (15–30s)

Run between tracks on the free tier with a clickable companion banner. The workhorse for incremental reach — full-message delivery, lowest CPM, the spine of the GeoLift test.

CPM ≈ $15–25 · completion 85–95%
Secondary · Attention

Video takeover

Served only when the app is visible and active, so views are genuinely viewable. Adds the visual proof — fit, fabric, real bodies — that audio alone can't show.

CPCV billing · CPM ~50–100% above audio
Tertiary · Trust

Podcast spots

Host-read or produced spots across the Spotify Audience Network. Borrows the host's relationship for a brand whose entire equity is trust, comfort and confidence — a natural fit we've circled before (iHeart, Oxford Road).

SAN reach · brand-safe genre targeting
Always-on · Tooling

Ads Manager 2026 toolkit

Split-testing on creative, automated ML bidding, and CPA optimization shipped in the 2026 release. We A/B the hook and offer instead of guessing, and let the bidder chase efficiency in real time.

05 — The demo: a 90-day proof

Designed as an
experiment, not a spray.

The whole point is a clean read. We run Spotify as a GeoLift test through Haus so the output is incremental revenue and channel-level iROAS we can defend to the board — not a vanity reach number.

Phase 0 · Weeks 1–2 · Setup

Wire it up & design the test

Connect Spotify Ads to Haus (already supported), define treatment vs. holdout geographies, lock the success metric (incremental orders & iROAS), and produce three audio scripts + one video cut around the body-confidence message.

  • GeoLift power analysis to size geos & budget for a readable result
  • First-party audience + lookalikes pushed via TTD/DV360 into SAX
  • Creative: 2–3 audio hooks for split-testing, 1 video, 1 podcast read
Phase 1 · Weeks 3–8 · In-market

Run, split-test, optimize

Push spend into treatment geos. Let automated bidding optimize to CPA while we split-test hooks and offers. Hold the holdout clean. Resist the urge to peek — GeoLift needs the full window.

  • Weekly creative & pacing review; no cross-contamination of geos
  • Audio as reach base; video + podcast layered for the in-demo core
  • Brand-lift / search-lift as supporting read alongside GeoLift
Phase 2 · Weeks 9–12 · Read & decide

Measure incrementality, feed cMMM

Haus reads true incremental lift vs. holdout. We get a defensible incremental iROAS, fold the result into cMMM, and make one call: scale, iterate, or shelve — with evidence either way.

  • Decision gate: incremental iROAS vs. pre-agreed threshold
  • If positive → graduate Spotify to an always-on diversification line
  • If flat → we've spent a contained test budget to retire the question

06 — What could go wrong

The honest
objections.

"Spotify is for Gen Z."

The 45–54 cohort is ~11% of a 380M ad-supported base — tens of millions of in-demo listeners. We win the argument with audience targeting and a GeoLift read, not vibes.

→ Geo + age/genre targeting; measure, don't assume

Audio attribution is murky.

No click ≠ no impact. That's exactly why we run GeoLift through Haus — incrementality vs. a holdout is the cleanest possible read for an upper-funnel audio channel.

→ Causal test, not last-click

It cannibalizes Meta budget.

The test budget is contained (~$80–105K) and ring-fenced. If incremental iROAS clears the bar, it's net-new growth; if not, we've retired the question cheaply.

→ Fixed, board-approved test envelope

Creative doesn't translate to audio.

Audio is a different craft than thumb-stopping social. We split-test 2–3 hooks and lean on podcast host-reads — the format our brand trust thesis was built for.

→ Split-test hooks; host-read trust play

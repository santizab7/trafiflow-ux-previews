01 — Who we are

An end-to-end platform that builds consumer brands as software.

Trafilea is vertically integrated — we design, develop, manufacture, market, sell, and deliver our own branded products, all on proprietary technology. We serve the unmet needs of middle-class women over 35 across evergreen intimates, beauty, and wellness categories, primarily in the U.S. (80% of sales) and tier-1 markets.

That integration compounds: every brand runs on the same data, tech, and growth engine, so each launch inherits the playbooks and unit economics of the last. 98% of sales come from owned brands, with profitable customer acquisition within 90 days. The result in 2025: $405M, profitably.

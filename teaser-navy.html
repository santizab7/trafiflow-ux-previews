01 — Who we are

We build, scale, and operate consumer brands as software.

Trafilea is a tech-driven house of brands. We design products, acquire customers, and run the entire commerce stack ourselves — creative, media, data, supply chain, and retention — on infrastructure we built in-house.

That vertical integration is the point. Every brand runs on the same operating system, so each new launch compounds the data, the playbooks, and the margin of the last one. The result in 2025: $405M in revenue, profitably.
